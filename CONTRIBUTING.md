# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this project! I'm glad you're taking the time to explore it.

This project is currently maintained independently, and I'm keeping development and maintenance focused on my own direction and goals. For this reason, I'm not accepting external contributions, pull requests, or new issues at this time.

You're more than welcome to explore the code, learn from it, and create your own fork for personal experimentation. I appreciate your interest and understanding!

Thank you for stopping by, and happy coding!
